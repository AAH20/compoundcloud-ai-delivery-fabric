# Unit economics contract

For every candidate, the fabric exposes:

- successful paid requests = demand × expected availability;
- revenue = successful paid requests × revenue per success;
- variable cost = inference + runtime + retrieval + network egress;
- gross profit = revenue − total cloud/infrastructure cost;
- gross margin = gross profit ÷ revenue;
- break-even demand = fixed platform cost ÷ contribution per request;
- capacity headroom = sustainable RPS ÷ required peak RPS.

The reference catalog is deliberately transparent and conservative enough for comparative testing, but it is not a provider quote. A real engagement replaces it with customer discounts, reserved capacity, staffing, support, depreciation, colocation, licensing, taxes, and measured token/cache distributions.

## Commercial decision gates

An architecture cannot win merely by being cheap. It must meet minimum quality, latency, availability, residency, budget, and capacity headroom. A failed gate remains visible in the output; the compiler never hides it inside a blended score.

## ROI workflow

1. Baseline the current cost per successful business outcome, not cost per token.
2. Compile at least three structurally different candidates.
3. Replay representative demand and failure scenarios.
4. Measure achieved quality and successful outcomes.
5. Include migration and operational labor in the investment case.
6. Promote only when measured incremental gross profit exceeds the risk-adjusted migration cost.
